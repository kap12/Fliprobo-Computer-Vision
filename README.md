We spoke with Craig earlier today, and we’re all of the mindset that this compensation from GME should be characterized as an “incentive” contractually.  I think that will help to avoid potential confusion on the part of regulators, central banks, or even the send-side agents that this is just incremental FX spread.  We should continue to settle with the consumer based on official rates when that’s mandated by the relevant regulator.

Since we are not paying GME any commissions on payout transactions, it makes sense to reflect this incentive as revenues (rather than negative commissions, which wouldn’t make sense).  That said, if we get these types of incentives from payout agents in the future, I think it makes sense to reduce APN commissions expense first, if there are any to begin with, and then take any excess incentives over those APN commissions to revenues as “subsidized consumer FX” revenues.
